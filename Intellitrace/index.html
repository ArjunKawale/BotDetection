QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about IntelliTrace architecture, privacy, and forensic scoring.

How is the final consensus verdict calculated?

IntelliTrace applies an equal 25% weighted average across 4 models: Semantic Coherence, Text Authenticity, Posting Cadence, and Behavioral Rhythm. Scores below 35% are classified as Likely Human, 35% to 65% as Uncertain, and above 65% as Likely Bot.

Does IntelliTrace send my data or scraping history anywhere?

No. All profile scrapings, post timelines, and computed verdict JSON files are stored locally in the application's UserData/ directory. There are zero background trackers, external analytics, or cloud telemetry.

Which social platforms are supported?

IntelliTrace natively supports Reddit user profiles (via local Playwright browser automation) and Bluesky profiles (via public AT Protocol decentralized feeds).

How accurate is the bot detection analysis?

By evaluating 4 distinct layers simultaneously—semantic intent, synthetic syntax, inter-post time intervals, and 24-hour circadian cycles—IntelliTrace effectively eliminates false alarms triggered by single metrics, such as a human who posts frequently.

What does an "Uncertain" verdict mean?

An "Uncertain" score (35%–65%) indicates conflicting signals, common with human-operated corporate accounts, community managers, or coordinated human astroturf rings that exhibit high post frequencies alongside natural human typing.

Can I re-analyze accounts without re-scraping?

Yes. Raw timeline archives are cached on disk as *_scraped.json. Clicking "Re-analyze Profile" in the dashboard re-computes all 4 models in under a second without making new network requests.
